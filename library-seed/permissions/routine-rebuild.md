# permission: routine-rebuild — replace a routine that does not produce its outcome

Rebuild only on the routine's OUTCOME evidence: its outcome series flat or absent for weeks
while its runs keep costing. A routine that produces its outcome is improved in place.
The candidate gets the old routine's settings without its secrets, so it cannot act in the
principal's name until the swap. Prove it on what it can reach: run it beside the old one and
compare their outcome series, never their own accounts of themselves.
One candidate per routine. Write its whole recipe (the outcome script included); the swap is the
operator's single decision, so the card's `why` names the evidence on both sides.
